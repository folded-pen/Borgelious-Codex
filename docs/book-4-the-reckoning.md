
# 🌅 Book the Fourth — *Of the Reckoning*

```
            .------------------------------.
            |     ~  THE  RECKONING  ~     |
            |    ( an itemised invoice )   |
            |..............................|
            |   the regret ........... ∞   |
            |   greasy bread ......... 1   |
            |   the great oath ....... 0   |
            |   your dignity ......... ?   |
            |..............................|
            |   TOTAL .......  the whole   |
            |                  of today    |
            |   no refunds.  no appeal.    |
            '-\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\-'
              ~  the morning sends its bill  ~
```

**IV:i** — The morning brings the Reckoning. It is not a punishment. It is **the invoice**.

**IV:v** — Thou shalt wake at the Hour of Regret and swear the Great Oath: *"Never Again."* Thou shalt break the Great Oath by sundown. This is not weakness. This is **the cycle**, and the cycle is holy.

**IV:ix** — Greasy bread is the only true medicine. For centuries the scholars searched for another. There is not another. There is only the bread.

**IV:xiii** — The hangover that waits and arrives at 3pm is the most fearsome of all — for it has had the entire day to plan.

**IV:xx** — Whosoever says *"I'm not even hungover"* before 11am has simply not yet been introduced to it. Be patient. It is on its way. It has your address.
